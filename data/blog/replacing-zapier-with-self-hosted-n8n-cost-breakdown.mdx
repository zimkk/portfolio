# Slashing $4,000/Month: The Complete Guide to Migrating from Zapier to Self-Hosted n8n

If your company runs 500,000 workflow tasks a month on Zapier with AI steps, your monthly invoice is easily **$3,800 to $5,200 per month**.

Every time a router branches, every time an email is parsed, and every time a filter condition evaluates, Zapier increments your task meter.

By migrating those workflows to a self-hosted n8n instance running on a $60/month AWS EC2 or Hetzner server:
- You pay **$0 per task execution**.
- You remove Zapier's 6MB payload size limits.
- Your sensitive customer data stays entirely within your private corporate VPC.

Here is the migration blueprint I use to seamlessly move enterprise clients from Zapier to n8n in under 7 days.

## Financial Comparison: 500,000 Tasks / Month

| Cost Item | Zapier Enterprise Tier | Self-Hosted n8n (AWS / Hetzner) |
|---|---|---|
| **Monthly Subscription** | $4,250.00 / month | $65.00 / month (Cloud Compute) |
| **Per-Task Overage Fees** | High ($0.015 / extra task) | **$0.00 (Unlimited Executions)** |
| **Data Privacy (HIPAA/GDPR)** | Shared Multi-Tenant Cloud | **100% Sovereign Private VPC** |
| **Annual Savings** | - | **$50,220.00 / year saved** |

## The 4-Step Migration Playbook

1. **Audit & Rank Zaps by Volume**: Export your Zapier activity logs and migrate the top 5 highest-volume zaps first to capture 80% of immediate savings.
2. **Translate Paths to n8n Switch Nodes**: Zapier's multi-zap paths translate cleanly into single, elegant n8n `Switch` and `If` nodes.
3. **Run in Parallel for 48 Hours**: Duplicate webhooks to both systems to verify 100% output equivalence before decommissioning Zapier.
4. **Enforce Local Secret Encryption**: Use n8n's built-in AES-256 encryption keys for API credentials.
